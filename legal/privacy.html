SplitTrip privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What SplitTrip is

SplitTrip is a small, invite-only app for sharing trip expenses. This page explains what information it handles.

What SplitTrip collects

  • From Google sign-in: your name, your email address and a unique account identifier. SplitTrip asks for nothing else from your Google account.
  • What you and the other people on a trip enter: trips, the names and email addresses of the people on them, expenses, how each expense is split, and payments.
  • A sign-in token: after you sign in, a random token is saved in your browser so you stay signed in. It expires after 30 days or when you sign out. The server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it.

What SplitTrip does not do

  • It does not sell or rent your information.
  • It does not show ads and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • It does not read your Google email, files or contacts.
  • It does not send you email.

How the information is used

Your name and email identify your account. Trips are stored so they are there when you sign in on another device and so the other people on a trip can see the shared expenses. Nothing is used for any other purpose.

Where it is stored and who else is involved

  • Accounts and trips are stored in a database hosted by Cloudflare. The app itself is served from GitHub Pages.
  • Google processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy.
  • To display the app and convert currencies, your browser loads code libraries, icons and exchange rates from jsDelivr, and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services can see your IP address when your browser asks them for these files. For exchange rates they also see which currency and date were requested. SplitTrip does not send them anything about you or your trips.

Who can see it

A trip is visible to the people who are members of that trip, and to nobody else. The person who runs SplitTrip could technically see stored data when maintaining the service, and will not share it with anyone else except where the law requires.

Backups and earlier copies

Before SplitTrip moved to its current database, its data was held in a different service. A read-only copy of that earlier data is kept for a short time after the move and is then deleted. The person who runs SplitTrip also keeps occasional backups of the current database. Backups are replaced over time, so data you delete may remain in a backup for a limited period before it is removed.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the account menu and choose Delete Account. This permanently deletes your sign-in details and your account. Trips are shared, so the expenses and payments recorded on trips, and your name and email in those trips' lists of people, stay so that everyone else's balances remain correct. If you want those removed as well, ask the person who invited you to SplitTrip. If you cannot use the app, ask them to delete your data.

Contact

SplitTrip is invite-only. For questions about this policy, ask the person who invited you to the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
