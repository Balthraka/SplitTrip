SplitTrip

SplitTrip is an app for sharing the cost of a trip. You create a trip, add the people who are on it, record who paid for what, and SplitTrip works out who owes whom so everyone can settle up with as few payments as possible.

What you can do

  • Create a trip and add the people travelling with you.
  • Record each expense in any of many currencies, and split it equally, by percentage or by exact amounts.
  • See each person's balance, converted to one currency using exchange rates.
  • Use Settle Up to see the simplest set of payments that clears the trip, and mark payments as paid.
  • Open the same trip on any device when you sign in.

Access

SplitTrip is a small personal project and is currently invite-only. You sign in with your Google account, so there is no separate password to manage.

Sign-in and your information

When you sign in, SplitTrip receives only your name, your email address and an account identifier from Google. It does not get access to your email, files, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

Read the SplitTrip privacy policy for what is stored and how to delete it.
